BHARATTRADE DISTRIBUTORS — PRICING POLICY (DEMO DOCUMENT)

DISCLAIMER: This is a fictional demonstration document for the BharatTrade Distributors demo business.
It does not represent any real company's policies or government regulations.

1. STANDARD WHOLESALE PRICING

All wholesale prices are quoted in Indian Rupees (INR / ₹) and are exclusive of GST unless stated otherwise.

Minimum Order Value (MOV): ₹5,000 per invoice for all trade partners.
Minimum Order Quantity: 10 units per SKU per order.

2. VOLUME DISCOUNT SCHEDULE

Orders exceeding 80 units per SKU qualify for a flat 5% trade discount on the listed MRP-based wholesale price.
Orders exceeding 200 units per SKU qualify for a 8% institutional discount, subject to advance payment.

3. GST APPLICABILITY

- Packaged Foods & Grocery: 5% GST (CGST 2.5% + SGST 2.5% for intra-state; IGST 5% for inter-state)
- Beverages: 12% GST
- Personal Care Products: 18% GST
- Household Essentials: 18% GST
- Commercial Stationery: 18% GST

All invoices clearly separate the GST-exclusive line total and the GST amount.
BharatTrade does not absorb GST for trade partners.

4. PRICE REVISION

Wholesale prices are reviewed quarterly. Price revisions are communicated 15 days in advance.
No retrospective price adjustments are permitted on delivered and accepted goods.

5. SPECIAL FESTIVE SEASON PRICING

During Diwali and Navratri festive season (October-November), select Packaged Foods and Beverages
categories may carry promotional pricing. Promotional discounts are clearly marked on invoices
and do not stack with volume discounts.
