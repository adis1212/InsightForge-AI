BHARATTRADE DISTRIBUTORS — RETURN AND REFUND POLICY (DEMO DOCUMENT)

DISCLAIMER: This is a fictional demonstration document for the BharatTrade Distributors demo business.
It does not represent any real company's policies.

1. RETURN ELIGIBILITY

Returns are accepted within 7 calendar days of delivery for the following reasons:
- Damaged or broken packaging during transit.
- Incorrect product delivered (wrong SKU or variant).
- Products past the printed expiry date at the time of delivery.
- Quality defects visible on inspection.

2. NON-RETURNABLE ITEMS

The following categories are non-returnable once accepted:
- Opened or used Personal Care products.
- Perishable Beverages (coconut water, mango pulp) after cold-chain handoff.
- Stationery items with broken seals.

3. RESTOCKING FEE

A restocking fee of 10% of the invoice value applies to all accepted returns
where the goods are in resalable condition but the return is initiated by the buyer
for reasons other than damage or defect.

4. REFUND PROCESS

Refunds are processed within 15 business days of return acceptance.
Refunds are credited to the original payment method or adjusted against outstanding invoices.
GST adjustments on returned goods are handled via credit notes as per CGST/SGST rules.

5. CANCELLATION POLICY

Orders may be cancelled before dispatch at no charge.
Orders cancelled after dispatch but before delivery incur a logistics charge of ₹200 per shipment.
Cancellation invoices are prefixed with 'C' (e.g., CINV-100045).
